# Candy Match

## What it is

“Candy Match” is an offline match-three puzzle game. Players swap neighboring sweets to reach score and heart goals, earn stars, and unlock new levels and candy looks. It is for players who want short puzzles they can play and replay at their own pace.

## Launch and onboarding

1. The system launch screen gives way directly to the “Journey” tab. There are no onboarding pages or first-launch permission prompts.
2. The screen opens with “Candy Match”, “A sweet little adventure”, and the total star count. The first card reads “DAILY CHALLENGE”, then “A fresh puzzle.” and “A little joy.” on separate lines, with “Play today” to open the daily board.
3. On a fresh install, the counters show “0 day streak” and “0 / 160”. “Your journey” begins at “Chapter 1 / 10”, “Jelly Garden”, with “1” available and the remaining level buttons showing locks. The bottom tabs are “Journey”, “Collection”, and “You”.
4. Tap “1” for the first campaign board or “Play today” for the daily board. The first campaign board shows “JELLY JOURNEY”, “Level 1”, “Moves” with “31”, “Score” with “0 / 662”, “Match sweets to fill the score bar”, and “Swap neighbors. Match three or more.” The question-mark button opens “A little guide” whenever help is needed.

- “Swap & match” — “Tap two neighboring sweets, or swipe. A move counts only if it makes a line of three or more. Invalid moves cost nothing.”
- “A special sweet” — “Four in a line creates a striped sweet that clears a row. Five creates a burst sweet that clears nearby tiles. Match it again to activate it, or swap two special sweets together.”
- “Reach both goals” — “Fill the score goal and collect the pink hearts shown at the top. One, two or three stars depend on the moves you have left.”
- “A helping hand” — “Burst clears a small area. Mix gives you a fresh board. Each tool starts with three uses; every three completed puzzles earns one more of each. Hints are always free.”
- “A puzzle every day” — “The daily seed and refill sequence are the same for everyone. Complete today's puzzle to grow your daily streak. You can replay without losing your best result.”

## Features

- “Your journey” has 160 campaign levels across 10 chapters. The first eight levels have score-only goals. Later levels also ask players to “Collect hearts”.
- “Daily challenge” offers a shared puzzle and refill sequence for each UTC date, with a “day streak” and completion calendar.
- “Seasonal collection” offers 12 immediately selectable puzzles per UTC month.
- “Swap & match” supports tapping and swiping on a seven-by-seven board. Matches can cascade. Four in a line can create a “Striped sweet” that clears a row. Five or more can create a “Burst sweet” that clears nearby sweets. Matching a special or swapping two adjacent specials can trigger their effects.
- “Hint” is unlimited. “Burst” and “Mix” start with three uses each. Every three distinct puzzle wins earn one more use of each.
- “Your best result is saved” reflects persistent best scores and star ratings. A win earns one to three stars based on moves remaining. Improving a rating increases the star total.
- “Your candy collection” offers four selectable looks, with three unlocked through star totals.
- “Gentle touch feedback”, optional “Daily reminders”, Home Screen widgets, a Lock Screen progress ring, and “How to play” are available through “You”.
- “Export file” and “Import file” let players move or combine saved results. “Save and close” lets them leave an unfinished puzzle and resume it later.
- “No lives to wait for. Play at your own pace.” Puzzles can be replayed without a life limit or waiting period.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- A locked level shows “Complete the previous level to open this one”. Tap “OK”, then win the next available level to advance. The chapter chevrons still let you browse ahead. The left chevron is disabled in the first chapter and the right chevron is disabled in the last.
- An invalid swap shows “Match three to make a move” in a “Candy Match” alert. Tap “OK” and make a horizontal or vertical match of at least three, or swap two adjacent specials. Rejected swaps use zero moves. “Hint” identifies a playable swap. The board can refresh automatically when it has no playable swap.
- Filling “Score” alone can leave a puzzle running. On a board with “Collect hearts”, clear enough pink hearts to meet that target as well before “Moves” reaches zero.
- At zero uses, “Burst” or “Mix” shows “Earn more tools by completing levels”. Tap “OK” and win more distinct puzzles to replenish both. “Hint” stays available. An armed “Burst” waits for a tile tap and can be toggled off by tapping “Burst” again.
- “Start again” asks for confirmation and replaces the current attempt. “Play again” deliberately opens the same puzzle. “Next level” is reserved for eligible campaign wins. After a loss, “Play again” starts a fresh attempt.
- “15 stars to unlock”, “40 stars to unlock”, and “80 stars to unlock” mark disabled look cards. Earn enough stars for “Use this look” to appear.
- The counters and calendar start with zero progress and no completed dates. The seasonal cards start with no earned stars. Win puzzles to populate them. Calendar dates are indicators, so tapping them has no effect.
- After a daily win, “Play today” stays available and opens a fresh replay. Replaying can improve the saved result. Each UTC date counts once toward the streak. The streak can still show yesterday’s run before today’s puzzle is complete. Missing a UTC day breaks the run.
- The daily entry switches at midnight UTC, and “Seasonal collection” switches at the first day of the UTC month. An unfinished daily or seasonal board may no longer be reachable from the current list after rollover. Saved stars from earlier sets still count.
- If notification access is denied, “Remind me to play” stays off and the app shows “Reminders are off. You can enable them in Settings.” Tap “OK” and enable notifications for Candy Match in system Settings. A request error can show “Reminders could not be enabled”.
- “Import file” expects a compatible Candy Match export. Invalid selections show a system error below the controls. Select a valid export to proceed. A local save failure can show “Could not save progress. Please check device storage.” Tap “OK” and check available device storage.

## Starter content and resume

A fresh install has the first campaign level, the current “Daily challenge”, all 12 current “Seasonal collection” puzzles, and the selected “Jelly Original” look. “Burst” and “Mix” each start at three uses. There are no prefilled wins, stars, streaks, or sample records.

Unfinished boards save on the device as they are played and through “Save and close”. Reopen the same puzzle from “Journey”, “Play today”, or “Seasonal collection” to resume it across launches while that entry is available. A normal cold launch opens “Journey”. Reopening a finished puzzle starts a fresh attempt. Exports carry records, tool-use history, and the selected look. Unfinished boards stay on the original device.

## Permissions

Notifications are optional. The system asks for permission to show alerts, play notification sounds, and set badges when the player first turns on “Remind me to play” under “You”. There is no custom usage-description string. The player can turn off “Remind me to play” to cancel scheduled reminders.

## Absent

Login or accounts, in-app purchase, ads, analytics, user-generated content, an account deletion flow, and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are all absent.

## Data and support

Progress, unfinished boards, and preferences stay on the device. Gameplay works offline. “Export file” lets the player choose where to save or share an export. “Import file” keeps the better saved scores and star ratings, combines completed records and tool-use history, and applies the more recently selected look. Existing unfinished boards stay in place. There is no on-screen Contact or Support control.

## Platform

iPhone and iPad, with a minimum of iOS 17.4 or iPadOS 17.4. iPhone supports portrait and both landscape orientations. iPad also supports upside-down portrait. The puzzle board places its goals and tools beside the grid when there is enough width.

The native interface is available in English, including US and UK variants, Arabic, Traditional Chinese, German, French, and Brazilian Portuguese. The display name is “Candy Match” across those localizations. UK English uses “Swap neighbours. Match three or more.”, “Select, then tap a neighbouring sweet”, and “Tap two neighbouring sweets, or swipe. A move counts only if it makes a line of three or more. Invalid moves cost nothing.” Dates follow the device locale. The daily and monthly puzzle schedules use UTC across regions, while reminders use local device time.
