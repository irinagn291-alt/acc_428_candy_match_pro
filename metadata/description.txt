Swap neighbouring sweets and set off cascades across a bright seven-by-seven board. Line up four to make a striped sweet, or five to create an area burst. Watch your moves as you collect pink hearts and aim for the best score.

Follow 160 levels through ten chapters. Try the daily challenge everyone shares, or discover twelve seasonal puzzles each month. Use free hints when you need them and earn Burst and Mix tools as you complete boards.

Stars unlock four candy looks. Your calendar tracks daily wins, and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your progress at a glance. The game saves unfinished boards on your device. Export completed results to a file to share them or import an earlier copy.

Play offline from the first launch. No account, ads, purchases or subscriptions.
